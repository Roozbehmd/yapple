WRONG NUMBER

PRIVACY POLICY
HOMESUPPORT

Wrong Number, by Roozbeh Moayyedian. Effective October 22, 2026.

The short version: Wrong Number has no accounts and no sign-up, and I don't run any servers that receive your information. The calls are pretend: they're made on your iPhone, by your iPhone. The free app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to show and measure it. The app also sends anonymous usage data, with nothing personal in it. One purchase unlocks the whole story and removes the ad.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR IPHONE

The app remembers your phone's colour, the day you installed it (that's the serial number), how many calls you've answered, which callers have already said what, the call that's waiting for you, and whether you've removed the ad. These are stored only on your iPhone and are deleted if you delete the app. Apart from the anonymous usage data below, they're never sent anywhere.

THE CALLS

The calls aren't real phone calls. Wrong Number never makes or receives calls, never uses your phone number, contacts or microphone, and never sends or receives messages. Each call is a local notification that the app books on your iPhone, at a random time; nothing about it goes through a server.

The callers' voices are synthesized on your iPhone, and the subtitles come with the app. Nothing you say or do during a call is recorded.

THE HINGE

On iPhone Duo the app reads the hinge angle, because opening and closing the phone is how you answer and hang up. It's used instantly on your iPhone, never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

ANONYMOUS USAGE DATA

The app sends anonymous usage data to TelemetryDeck: which calls are reached, how many numbers are dialled, and whether the story has been unlocked. It contains no personal data (no name, email, phone number, contacts or advertising identifier, only a random ID that TelemetryDeck makes up for this app), it isn't used for tracking or advertising, and it's used only to improve the app.

ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)

The free app shows one banner ad, served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), your IP address and the approximate location it gives away, information about the ads you see and tap, and diagnostic information. How Google uses information from apps that use its services explains more.

iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you say no, you still see an ad, just a less relevant one, and you can change your answer under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalized, and Ad privacy choices, under UNLOCK, lets you change it.

YOUR CHOICES

  • Say no to tracking, or change your ad privacy choices, as above.
  • Buy Unlock Story & Remove Ads once and the app stops loading ads altogether.
  • Turn notifications off and calls only ring while the app is open.

PURCHASES

The Unlock Story & Remove Ads purchase, and any offer code redeemed for it, is processed entirely by Apple. I never see your payment details. The app only learns from the App Store whether you own it, so it can keep your line open, stop showing the ad and restore the purchase on your other devices.

CHILDREN

Wrong Number is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

CHANGES
